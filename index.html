How it works
How Potential turns into stats

Each point adds 0.8% of the stat, and every 4th point adds another 4%. A stat at 20 is about 39% higher than at 0.

The sources don't say whether those two parts add or multiply. Added gives 36%; multiplied gives 1.16 × 1.20 = 1.392, which matches the "about 39%" the sources quote, so the planner multiplies by default. This is an assumption until checked against a real stat screen. Switch it under Settings and data. Either way, the point that lands on a multiple of 4 is worth about six ordinary points.

Blue bonuses and breakpoints

Effective Potential is the displayed number plus the blue bonus. Star Up ranks 5, 6 and 7 add +1, +1 and +2 to all six stats; a +10 held item adds +2 to one stat (Giant Tortoise Shell adds +2 to both defenses).

With a blue bonus of the breakpoints land on displayed . Displayed stops at 20, so with +2 the last breakpoint is 18. The blue bonus keeps counting past 20: displayed 20 with +4 is 24 effective, which reaches another ×4 breakpoint, and 20 with +6 is 26 (MOANIIMO). The planner and Optimizer count it the same way.

Which stat a hit uses

DPS and Break: max(ATK, 0.85 × BREAK). Support, Regen and Heal: 10.5 × level.

Only the larger of ATK and 0.85 × BREAK counts, so points in the smaller one add no hit damage. Popota hits with ATK at equal Potential (ratio 0.85). Bolty (1.04) and Blazen (1.06) sit near the line but still favor BREAK. Damage is attack minus the target's defense, so a few percent of attack matters more against tough targets.

Skill effects with a cap

ATK effects stop at 800, REGEN effects at 500, HP effects at 16,000.

These cap a named skill or trait, not the stat itself. Every 100 points of raw stat adds a fixed amount until the cap; Crazy Bubbles goes from 16% to 28% at +1.5% per 100 ATK. Enter your raw stat and the planner stops that stat where it should reach the cap.

Points, materials and level gates

Awakening count: 5 (level 1 to 44), 10 (45 to 54), 20 (55 to 59), 30 (60 to 64), 40 (65 to 69), 55 (70), plus 5, 8 and 10 at Star Up ranks 5, 6 and 7. Level 60 at rank 6 gives 43.

Cost per point in a stat: points 1 to 4 take 1 to 4 Star Dust, 5 to 8 take 1 to 4 Star Sand, 9 to 13 take 1 to 5 Starcryst Essence, and 14 to 20 take 5 Essence each.

Level gates: points 11 and 12 need level 40, 13 to 15 need 50, 16 to 19 need 55, and 20 needs 60.

Known gaps
  • Level gates and material costs may count total Potential or only Acquired points. The planner assumes total; one in-game check settles it.
  • Cap stopping points are estimates. Flat bonuses such as Resonance stars don't scale with Potential, so the estimate runs a little high.
  • Not yet confirmed: Chirpi's Band Member ATK scaling, Hexxin's Prank cap of 500, and how strong Water Spirit's REGEN effect is on Skippy, Pranky and Glacy.
  • Fennelun and Soleon aren't listed in the AniimoTools Aniilog and have no element icon here.
  • Upcoming Aniimo (#084 to #093) have no advice yet. #083 is skipped in the dex itself.
